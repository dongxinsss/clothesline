# Share Clothesline / 拾光绳分享素材

Repository: https://github.com/dongxinsss/clothesline
Downloads: https://github.com/dongxinsss/clothesline/releases

Use only the files under `docs/media` when sharing project visuals. These contain
new synthetic examples. Do not use private clipboard history or old native
screenshots. The cover/social card is marketing artwork; the product captures
show the browser demo and are labeled accordingly.

## 简体中文 · 短介绍

拾光绳：把截图挂起来，把工作接下去。

给 macOS 和 Windows 做了一个小小的桌面收纳工具：截图、文字和日程先挂在一条绳上，用完收进可拖动的小篮子。历史会保留，主题、字号和空闲收起时间可调。Go + 系统 WebView，本地保存，MIT 授权。

目前为 0.4.1 预览版：macOS 有本机验证，Windows 已交叉编译、尚待真机测试。欢迎试用、Star 或提交可复现的问题。

## English · Short introduction

Clothesline — hang onto a screenshot, then get back to your work.

A small local shelf for screenshots, notes, and schedules on macOS and Windows.
Fold it into a draggable basket, keep your history, and adjust themes, fonts, and
idle collapse. Built with Go and the OS webview, under MIT.

0.4.1 is a preview. macOS has local checks; Windows is cross-compiled and still
needs real-host testing. Try it, share it, or help with a reproducible bug report.
